Solmar Heat is a top-down open-world crime game set in Solmar, a sun-soaked coastal city with five districts: Marina Bay, Old Town, Downtown, Palm Heights and the Industrial Docks. You play Rico, a newcomer driver working for the fixer Mama Luz. Explore the city on foot or behind the wheel, steal five kinds of car — sedan, taxi, pickup, sport and van, each with its own speed and handling — dodge traffic and pedestrians, and stay one step ahead of the Solmar police as your wanted level climbs from one star to five.

Six chained story missions take Rico from his first stolen car to the throne of the city: a courier run against the clock, a pickup across town, a shootout with a rival crew at Dock 7, a getaway with the whole police force in pursuit, and a final showdown at the Marina. Fists and a scavenged pistol, a live minimap, health, cash, autosave and synthesized engine, siren and mission sounds round out the heat. Everything is original — city, characters, story and art, drawn in plain Canvas 2D with no external files.

## How to play

- **WASD / Arrow keys** — walk and drive (Space is the handbrake)
- **E** — enter, steal or leave a car (stealing a car with its driver inside raises your wanted level)
- **F** — punch · **G or mouse click** — shoot (once you find a pistol)
- **M** — start the next story mission · **P / Esc** — pause · **N** — mute

## Run it

node serve.ts
